Covers the 15-node demo and the autonomous verification goal:

  historical build;

## 1. 15-node demo (`bin/sd-demo15`)

Four overlapping s_groups, two gateways, 15 nodes, one container each.
- `demo/config/sd15.config` is the single source for groups, discovery seeds and services.
- **10/10 checks pass on three consecutive fresh clusters.**

| | SD Erlang 19.3 | stock OTP (same base) |
|---|---|---|
| visible links | **35** | 105 (full mesh) |
| links formed by SD's own meshing (beyond the 14 seeded) | 21 | — |
| max links per node | 10 | 14 |

Checks cover:
- static membership;
- intra-group meshing from gateway-only seeds, and no visible cross-group links;
- `worker_pool` as three different processes in three groups, plus name
  convergence on every member;
- client → gateway → worker routing that creates no new links;
- member crash and gateway crash, with isolation and recovery;
- moving `edge4` between groups.

## 2. (B) What SD Erlang is: `spec/`

Primary sources, downloaded, hashed and read (`manifests/spec-sources.yml`):
- RELEASE D3.3 (TR-2014-004): the 15-operation operational semantics and the
  QuickCheck method;
- Li & Thompson AST'14: the eqc_statem approach, and the spec/implementation
  bugs RELEASE found;
- JPDC'16: the design contract;
- TOPLAS'17: the final summary.

- `SD_ERLANG_SPEC.md` separates three layers: **S** (design contract),
  **M** (formal semantics) and **I** (the 19.3 code). It states each of the 15
  operations under M and I, and classifies every known difference.
- `spec/model/sd_model.erl` is the D3.3 semantics, **executable**: every rule
  and auxiliary function. `ERRATA.md` records 7 interpretation choices where
  the printed rules are ambiguous. 22 tests pass, including 200 seeded random
  walks checking the D3.3 partition property.
- `spec/deviations.yml` is the single register a human owns. It holds the
  divergences RELEASE recorded (R1–R7) and those found here (below).

## 4. Findings in the 19.3 reference

| ID | Finding | How found | Class |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | `add_nodes(G,[Caller])` returns `{ok,G,[]}` without a membership check | PBT, shrunk to 1 command | I-behaviour (value) |
| I2 | `remove_nodes/2` removes the *intersection* and returns `ok` | PBT campaign | I-behaviour (**state**) |
| O5 | a group name registered while `global` is syncing a new group can be **permanently** missing on some members | demo15 repro 3/3; PBT shrunk to 2 commands; `sd_pbt_o5`: **3/20 never converge in 120 s** | defect |
| O6 | a name registered before `add_nodes/2` can miss a newly added member | campaign 1/10 runs | defect |
| Q1 | after `new_s_group`, one member's `global` occasionally never syncs (within 10 s) | campaigns 2/20 runs | defect; suspected = RELEASE R3 (60 s "nodeup never came" path) |
| Q2 | `remove_nodes(g1,[C])` wipes `global`'s knowledge of C in *another* group shared with C | campaign, then **deterministic repro 5/5** (`sd_pbt_q2`) | defect; **root cause located**: unconditional `disconnect_nodes/1` in `remove_nodes_check` |
| O1 | `remove_nodes` keeps TCP links | demo | **by design** (JPDC'16: "keeps its existing connections"); reclassified from report 01 |
| O2/O3/O4 | cross-group links are hidden; links made before sharing a group stay hidden; lookup misses search other groups | probes | I-behaviour; O3 needs a human decision |
| H1 | "hidden initiator doesn't interconnect" | — | **retracted**: harness artifact (zombie BEAMs exhausting pids; fixed with `--init`) |

**O5/O6 mechanism.** The first TLA+ model (snapshot-vs-known window) reproduced
the *shape* of O5. Code reading then ruled it out for 19.3, because
registration and sync share the `?GLOBAL_RID` lock.

The refined hypothesis: every completed sync runs `unregister_foreign_names/1`,
while `s_group` updates `global`'s own-group view asynchronously, so a lagging
node deletes group names it just received.
- `SGroupForeignGC.tla`: TLC finds the violation, and verifies two candidate
  fixes (synchronous membership hand-off; GC only on removal).
- Runtime confirmation is pending: §6.

## 5. Oracle self-agreement (L2) and free-node equivalence (L5)

**L5, free-node equivalence** (20 s_group-free sequences over 14 nodes:
connections plus the `global` registry):

| comparison | identical |
|---|---|
| SD 19.3 vs SD 19.3 (oracle self-agreement) | **20/20** |
| SD 19.3 vs stock OTP from the exact parent commit | **18/20** |

The only differences are new finding **F1**: on free nodes a failed
`global:register_name/2` returns `{no, name_or_pid_already_registered}` on SD
and `no` on stock. Every observed *state* matched in 20/20. F1 breaks S2 and the
`yes | no` contract of `global`; the port policy is a human decision
(recommendation: match stock).

**L2, differential self-agreement** (30 sequences, 292 commands, 19.3 vs its own
golden traces):
- At a 500 ms settle window: 26/30 and 27/30. Raising the window above
  `global`'s locker retry interval (3.5 s) was needed.
- The 3.5 s reruns then exposed a harness crash on `{badrpc, _}` during
  observation: some sequence makes a node unreachable or crashes `s_group`.
  The harness now records `badrpc` as an observation, and
  `sd_crash_hunt` is locating the sequence.

## 6. OTP 29.1.2: `spec/PORT_OTP29_FEASIBILITY.md`

- Upstream drift since the SD base: `global.erl` +1,470/−346 (48 commits) and
  `net_kernel.erl` +2,003/−608 (118 commits). Re-applying the 19.3 patch
  mechanically isn't viable for `global.erl`.
- **Key conflict:** since OTP 25, `prevent_overlapping_partitions` (default on)
  makes `global` *disconnect* nodes to keep fully connected partitions.
  Overlapping s_groups are overlapping partitions by design.
- **Key opportunity:** modern `global` already consults `global_group` through
  a narrow interface (`participant/1`, `member/1`, `group_configured/0`,
  `publish/2`; 20 call sites).
- Recommended design B: an `s_group` topology provider on that seam, plus a
  separate per-group namespace server. Decisions for a human are listed there
  (O3, O5/Q-fixes, partition-prevention scope, `global_group` coexistence).
